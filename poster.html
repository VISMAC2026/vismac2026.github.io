Call for Posters

VISMAC 2026 - International Summer School on Machine Vision

Siena, Italy - 21-25 September 2026

Call for Posters

The VISMAC 2026 International Summer School aims to provide a stimulating opportunity for Ph.D. students and young researchers. Participants will have the chance to present the results of their research, interact with their scientific peers, and engage with computer vision experts in a friendly and constructive environment.

All accepted and registered students as well as sponsors are highly encouraged to submit a poster to present their research activity. Suitable space will be reserved at the Santa Chiara Lab (University of Siena) for displaying the posters during dedicated sessions.

The electronic version of the posters will also be made available on the VISMAC website.

Topics of Interest

Topics of interest include, but are not limited to:

Image and video understanding
3D computer vision
Generative computer vision
Action recognition and egocentric vision
Embodied-AI and robotics
Continual learning
Image forensics
Computer vision applications and systems

Poster Guidelines

The poster should be submitted in a printable PDF format. For your poster, a board will be provided which measures 1000mm tall by 700mm wide. You are required to print and bring the poster with you to the school. Push tacks or velcro adhesive will be provided by the local arrangements team to mount your poster to the board.

Submission Deadline

Please submit your poster by 14 September 2026 by sending it to the following email: vismac2026@gmail.com.

Submission Rules

Submissions not satisfying the following guidelines may be rejected:

  • Title and author list clearly visible;
  • Poster size: max 1000mm tall x 700mm wide (Portrait orientation).

For more information or inquiries regarding the poster session, please contact: vismac2026@gmail.com
